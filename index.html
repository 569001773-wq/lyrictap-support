Quick answers

LyricTap FAQ

01

What is LyricTap?

LyricTap is an iPhone and iPad lyric sync app for creating timed lyrics, exporting LRC and SRT files, and making lyric videos.

02

Is LyricTap free to use?

Yes. LyricTap offers free manual lyric syncing. The browser studio is free, runs locally, and requires no account; AI Auto-Sync is an optional faster workflow in the app.

03

How does LyricTap sync lyrics?

LyricTap can sync lyrics manually as you tap each line, or use AI Auto-Sync to create a full editable first-pass timeline from your audio and lyrics.

04

Which lyric files can LyricTap export?

LyricTap exports LRC files for synchronized lyrics, SRT files for video editors, and plain TXT lyrics.

05

Can LyricTap create lyric videos?

Yes. LyricTap turns synced lyrics into portrait or landscape lyric videos using three visual templates.

06

Which song languages does LyricTap support?

LyricTap supports lyric syncing for English, Japanese, and Chinese songs.

07

Does LyricTap work with Suno or Udio songs?

Yes. You can import audio exported from Suno or Udio like other supported audio files. LyricTap is not officially integrated with or affiliated with either service.

08

Does the browser studio upload my audio?

No. The free browser studio processes audio and lyric timing locally on your device and does not upload the selected song.
