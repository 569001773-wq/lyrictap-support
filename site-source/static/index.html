Quick answers

LyricTap FAQ

01

What is LyricTap?

LyricTap is an iPhone and iPad lyric sync app for creating timed lyrics, exporting LRC and SRT files, and making lyric videos.

02

Is LyricTap free to use?

The browser studio is free, runs locally, and requires no account. AI Auto-Sync in the iOS app uses paid credits. Check the App Store for current app pricing and availability.

03

How does LyricTap sync lyrics?

LyricTap can sync lyrics manually as you tap each line, or use AI Auto-Sync to create a full editable first-pass timeline from your audio and lyrics.

04

Which lyric files can LyricTap export?

LyricTap exports LRC files for synchronized lyrics, SRT files for video editors, and plain TXT lyrics.

05

Can LyricTap create lyric videos?

Yes. LyricTap turns synced lyrics into portrait or landscape lyric videos using the available visual styles.

06

Which song languages does LyricTap support?

LyricTap supports lyric syncing for English, Japanese, and Chinese songs.

07

Does LyricTap work with Suno or Udio songs?

LyricTap can open supported local audio that you are permitted to use, including previously exported tracks. It cannot import a Suno or Udio account link. Udio currently reports downloads disabled; check its current policy. LyricTap is not affiliated with or endorsed by either service.

08

Does the browser studio upload my audio?

No. The free browser studio processes audio and lyric timing locally on your device and does not upload the selected song.

09

How does AI lyric translation work?

After a successful full-song AI sync, choose a target language to translate saved lyrics. Edit translations line by line, display bilingual lyrics in the player, and export original, translation or bilingual content. Translation does not consume another AI sync credit.

10

Can I download vocals and instrumentals?

Yes. A completed full-song AI sync includes separated vocal and instrumental tracks. Save them to your library before their temporary links expire.
