Website privacy & your editor data

English

The free browser editor processes your selected audio and lyrics on this device. It never uploads audio, filenames or lyric text. Lyrics and timing progress are saved in this browser’s local storage so you can resume; audio files are not saved. Use “Delete saved progress” in the editor to remove the saved draft. Further edits create a new local draft. Browser data may be cleared by private browsing, device settings or storage limits.

Optional visitor analytics is currently disabled. Choosing “Allow” in Analytics preferences lets Plausible count page visits, referring sites, editor use, timeline builds, file exports and App Store clicks. Events include the page language, the export format or the App Store campaign identifier when applicable. The provider derives approximate country from the network request. No audio, lyrics, filenames, URL queries, user accounts or persistent visitor identifiers are sent by our adapter. You can withdraw consent at any time; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are respected.

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process connection information to deliver and secure it. Apple handles App Store visits and downloads under its own policy. AI processing in the iOS app is separate: it uploads data only when you choose that app feature and consent. See the app privacy policy.

日本語

現在、任意のアクセス解析は無効です。許可すると、Plausible がページの閲覧、参照元サイト、おおよその国、表示言語、エディタの利用、タイムラインの作成、ファイルの書き出し、App Store リンクのクリックを集計します。書き出し形式やリンクのキャンペーン識別子も対象です。音声・歌詞・ファイル名は収集しません。設定から同意を撤回できます。iOSアプリのAI処理は別の機能であり、アプリのプライバシーポリシーをご確認ください。

简体中文

免费网页工具不上传音频、文件名或歌词。歌词和打轴时间保存在当前浏览器，音频不保存。可点击“Delete saved progress”删除已保存进度；后续编辑会生成新的本地草稿。

目前未启用可选访问统计。选择「允许」后，Plausible 会统计访问页面、来源网站、大致国家或地区、页面语言、编辑器使用、时间轴生成、文件导出及 App Store 点击。导出事件包含文件格式，商店点击包含推广链接的标识。歌词、文件名和音频始终保留在浏览器内。可随时撤回同意。iOS 应用的 AI 云端处理属于独立功能，详见应用隐私政策。
